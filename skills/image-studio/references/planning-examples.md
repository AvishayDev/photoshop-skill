# Example plans (adapt; never paste verbatim)

## "Create tiger in forest, realism style" (see pipelines/tiger_in_forest.py)

1. Search the pattern library and knowledge base for tiger, fur, stripes, forest and natural light.
2. Research tiger references on the internet: side-view silhouette, stripe layout, white markings, ear
   spots, tail tip; record sources.
3. Learn tiger patterns from a reusable-licence photo: stripe period and coverage, palette, outline
   (`learn_pattern`); save them (`save_pattern`).
4. Research forest structure: depth layers, trunk spacing, canopy, undergrowth, haze, light shafts.
5. Define the composition: 16:10, tiger walking on the lower third, eye level near its back, key light
   from the front-left.
6. Paint the light through the canopy and the hazy far forest wall.
7. Build the forest in four depth layers, paler and smaller with distance.
8. Paint the forest floor with perspective texture, a worn path and grass.
9. Generate the tiger from the figure recipe and the learned stripe pattern, lit by the key light.
10. Blend the tiger into the scene: contact shadow, cast shadow, rim light.
11. Add god rays and depth haze so the subject separates from the background.
12. Put foreground grass in front of the paws.
13. Grade with adjustment layers, vignette and grain.
14. Render previews, inspect scale, light direction, grounding and edges; fix and re-run.
15. Export PNG/JPEG + layered ORA, report limitations, save improved patterns.

## "Create a peacock in the ocean" (see pipelines/peacock_in_ocean.py)

1. Search for peacock images; 2. find peacock borders (silhouette); 3. understand the peacock pattern
(neck scales, barred wings, train eyespots); 4. understand peacock lighting from other light directions;
5. search for ocean images; 6. ocean borders (horizon, shallow edge); 7. objects in oceans; 8. patterns
of objects in the ocean (submerged legs, ripples); 9. light, shadow and reflection of objects in water;
10. sun/moon light on the ocean (glitter path, Fresnel); 11. generate the ocean scene; 12. generate the
peacock from the learned patterns; 13. set the peacock in the ocean; 14. blend its lighting; 15. blend
its shadows/ripples; 16. blend its reflection; 17. final touches and inspection; 18. return the image.

## "Crop this photo to a square for Instagram" (simple requests still get 10+ steps)

1. Inspect the document's dimensions and resolution.
2. Identify the subject's position and the important content to keep.
3. Decide the target: 1:1, 1080x1080 export.
4. Calculate the largest square crop that keeps the subject well placed.
5. Check the proposed framing for cut-off limbs/text and headroom.
6. Create a checkpoint.
7. Apply the crop.
8. Render a preview and check edges and composition.
9. Verify the canvas is exactly square; export resized to 1080x1080.
10. Verify the exported file and report the paths.
